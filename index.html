How to Play FocusSphere

1. The Objective: Test and measure your visual tracking focus over extended periods (2 to 20 minutes) without distraction.

2. The 30-Second Lag: When you start, 1 unique ball appears. For exactly 30 seconds, it bounces freely alone. Memorize its starting appearance and path!

3. Multiplication: After 30 seconds, lookalike balls spawn and multiply progressively until exactly 30 balls bounce around.

4. The Confusion Challenge: To prevent color alone from giving away the target, multiple balls share similar color palettes and appearance hues.

5. Instant Stop & Reveal: The exact second your timer expires, motion freezes. You must click on the ball you tracked. The game instantly reveals your picked ball (Red if wrong / Green if correct) and highlights the true original ball in Green.

6. CSV Export: All game scores and stats are saved locally and can be exported anytime as a CSV report.
